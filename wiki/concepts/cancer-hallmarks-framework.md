# Cancer Hallmarks Framework

## Definition
The hallmarks of cancer conceptualization, developed by Hanahan and Weinberg (2000, 2011) and refined through 2026, organizes the mechanistic complexity of cancer into four distinct conceptual dimensions. It posits that cancer requires the acquisition of specific functional capabilities (hallmarks) that are enabled by underlying phenotypic characteristics and supported by recruited cells in the tumor microenvironment, all operating within a systemic organismal context.

## The Four Dimensions (as of 2026)

### Dimension 1: Nine Acquired Functional Capabilities (Hallmarks)
| Hallmark | Core mechanism | Key example |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Sustaining proliferative signaling | Oncogene activation | [[KRAS]] ~50% CRC |
| Inactivating growth suppressors | TSG mutation | [[APC]], [[TP53]], RB |
| Resisting programmed cell death | Anti-apoptotic upregulation | BCL-2, BCL-XL, MCL-1 |
| Establishing replicative immortality | TERT reactivation / ALT | TERT promoter mutations |
| Inducing/accessing vasculature | Angiogenesis + vascular co-option | VEGFA, ANGPT2 |
| Deregulating cellular metabolism | Warburg effect + hybrid metabolism | Lactate symbiosis |
| Unlocking phenotypic plasticity | Intra/trans-lineage state switching | EMP, CSC states |
| Evading immune destruction | T cell exhaustion + TME immunosuppression | PD-1/PD-L1 axis |
| Activating invasion and metastasis | Metastatic cascade, EMP, CIN | Premetastatic niche |

### Dimension 2: Five Enabling Phenotypic Characteristics
These are not hallmarks themselves but traits that facilitate hallmark acquisition:
1. **Loss of genomic integrity** — CIN, point mutations, ecDNA, chromothripsis
2. **Non-mutational epigenetic reprogramming** — DNA methylation, histone code
3. **Tumor-promoting inflammation** — TAMs/TANs co-opting wound-healing programs
4. **Innervation** — autonomic/somatosensory axons modulating hallmarks *(new as of ~2023)*
5. **Polymorphic microbiomes** — gut and tissue microbiomes modulating immune evasion *(new as of ~2022)*

### Dimension 3: Seven Hallmark-Conveying TME Cell Classes
All can adopt both tumor-promoting and tumor-suppressing substates:
1. [[Cancer cells]] (including CSC spectrum)
2. [[Endothelial cells and pericytes]]
3. [[CAFs]] (myoCAFs and iCAFs)
4. [[TAMs]] and tumor-associated neutrophils
5. Additional immune cells (Tregs, DCs, NK, Bregs)
6. Neurons and axonal projections
7. Senescent cells (SASP-driven)

### Dimension 4: Systemic Interactions
Tumors as systemic disease; "clouds of complexity":
- **Aging** — accumulation of oncogenic mutations, loss of immune fitness, senescent cell SASP
- **Obesity** — leptin/adiponectin imbalance; sustains proliferative signaling, angiogenesis, inflammation
- **Innervation** (also systemic)
- **Microbiome** (also systemic)
- **Cachexia**, **environmental agents** (smoking, microplastics, pollution)

## Relevance to CRC Metastasis
- [[KRAS]] (~50% CRC) is uniquely associated with ALL 9 hallmarks and tumor-promoting inflammation
- [[APC]] loss (WNT activation) is the canonical CRC-initiating event; links to growth suppressor hallmark
- [[TP53]] loss (~60% mCRC) enables both genome instability and phenotypic plasticity/dedifferentiation
- Gut [[microbiome]] is the most studied microbiome in cancer; directly relevant to CRC pathogenesis
- The metastasis hallmark (invasion, EMP, premetastatic niche) is the primary driver of CRC mortality

## Key Debates / Contradictions
- Hallmarks are not strictly linearly acquired; temporal order varies by tumor type and oncogenic driver
- Some pediatric cancers have no identifiable oncogenic mutations — purely epigenetic governance
- Normal tissues are replete with asymptomatic KRAS/TP53 mutations → mutations alone don't explain tumorigenesis
- EMT is now reframed as "epithelial-mesenchymal plasticity (EMP)" — hybrid states dominate, not discrete transitions

## Therapeutic Implications: Hallmark Co-targeting
The paper's major corollary: targeting multiple independent hallmarks simultaneously makes adaptive resistance harder. Proven combinations:
- VEGF/VEGFR inhibitors + immune checkpoint inhibitors (anti-PD-1/PD-L1)
- Bevacizumab + olaparib (PARP inhibitor)
Future: ICI + TME immunosuppressive cell reprogramming (CAFs, TAMs, TANs)

## Open Questions
- Will additional hallmarks be identified with general applicability across cancers?
- Can phenotypic plasticity be therapeutically inhibited?
- How do innervation effects vary across cancer types, including CRC?
