## Entities

### Genes & Proteins
- [[EMP1]] — Tight-junction tetraspan protein; top marker of HRCs; tracks metastasis-initiating cells at invasion fronts and micrometastases; expression driven by MAPK/AP-1 axis (2 sources)
- [[KRAS]] — Most frequently mutated oncogene in CRC (~50%); drives MAPK-high/WNT-low metastatic state; three classes of (K)RAS inhibitors; EGFR adaptive resistance dominates in CRC; YAP/TEAD and EMT as non-genetic resistance (4 sources)

### Metastatic Mechanisms

- [[Epithelial-Mesenchymal Plasticity (EMP)]] — Dynamic hybrid epithelial-mesenchymal states driving CRC invasion; HRC partial-EMT state driven by MAPK/AP-1; EMT also a non-genetic resistance mechanism to KRAS inhibition (4 sources)
